# Yard Safety Procedure

*Synthetic/demo document created for a portfolio project. This is not a real operational
manual and does not represent the procedures of any actual company or terminal.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure establishes minimum safety requirements for all personnel working in
the container yard, including terminal operators, crane operators, straddle carrier
drivers, truck drivers, and visiting contractors. Compliance is mandatory for all
individuals entering yard operational areas.

## 2. Personal Protective Equipment

All personnel in operational yard areas must wear a high-visibility vest, safety
footwear, and a hard hat at all times. Personnel working near active crane operations
must additionally wear hearing protection when within twenty meters of running
equipment. Visitors and contractors are issued visitor PPE at the gate and must be
escorted by terminal staff while in operational areas.

## 3. Restricted Zones

The area directly beneath an operating crane or spreader is a restricted zone and no
personnel may enter it while the crane is in motion. Restricted zones are marked with
yellow-and-black boundary markings and, where practical, physical barriers. Reefer
rack access is restricted to authorized reefer technicians and operators performing
scheduled monitoring checks. Any breach of a restricted zone must be reported to the
yard safety officer regardless of whether an incident occurred.

## 4. Container Stacking Limits

Maximum stacking height is determined by container weight, terminal ground
conditions, and equipment reach, and is set by the yard planning system for each yard
block. Operators must not override system-assigned stacking limits without written
authorization from the yard safety officer. Damaged containers, as classified under
the Damaged Container Handling Procedure, may not be stacked above ground level under
any circumstances.

## 5. Crane and Equipment Operation Safety

Crane operators must complete a pre-shift equipment check before beginning operations,
covering brakes, spreader locking mechanisms, and warning systems. Any fault
identified during the pre-shift check must be logged and the equipment taken out of
service until cleared by maintenance. Ground personnel must maintain visual or radio
contact with the crane operator at all times during active lifts; if contact is lost,
the lift must be paused immediately.

## 6. Hazard Reporting

Any hazard — including damaged pavement, malfunctioning equipment, obstructed
walkways, or near-miss incidents — must be reported to the yard safety officer within
one hour of observation using the terminal operating system's hazard reporting module.
Hazards posing immediate danger must be reported by radio immediately in addition to
the system entry, and the affected area cordoned off until resolved.

## 7. Incident Response

In the event of an injury or serious equipment incident, the nearest staff member must
immediately halt all operations in the affected area, call for medical assistance if
required, and notify the shift operations manager. The area must be preserved as-is
(except where required for immediate safety) until the yard safety officer has
completed an initial assessment. A full incident report is required within
twenty-four hours of any injury or serious near-miss.
